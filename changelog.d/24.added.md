Contract the rebuilt column layer. The column-expression and protocol contracts are validated against each backend's own `ColumnTypeSupport` tables: an explicit declaration is backend-independent, inference is compared with the backend's `suggested_column_types()` answer, and a backend refusal (`None`) skips rather than fails. New guarantees pin the completeness of the common entries (a canonical `COMMON_TYPES` list now lives in `column_helpers.py`), class-or-`None` answers, constructibility, the absence of a value-family label, and the `UseColumnType` escape hatch past a refusal. Core-level `Model.c` operation gates exercise the surface through the field proxy: each value family's operations are reachable and produce expressions, and the ones a family does not offer refuse — `AttributeError` for a missing attribute, `TypeError` where the interpreter itself refuses a family that never declared arithmetic.
