Fix two silent failures that left the suite reporting success while testing nothing. 44 protocol references could not be resolved -- the bare names `JoinSupport` and `SchemaSupport`, which carry no dot for the resolver to split, and `IndexSupport`, which stopped resolving when core's `protocols.py` became a `protocols/` package -- so the full-text-search compatibility test skipped all 11 of its parametrisations and every join test skipped, on every backend.
